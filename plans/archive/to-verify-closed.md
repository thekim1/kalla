The rows of [`../to-verify.md`](../to-verify.md) that are resolved or dropped, under their original numbers and
headings; rows struck through are from before the status column existed. A resolved row's result is also in its
design document's section. Document names are relative to `plans/`. These rows no longer bind as checks.

How the register grew (its header until 2026-11-08):

> One register of the "To verify" items of every detail document (collected in the consistency pass, detail step 11).
> The per-document "To verify" sections stay the source with the full context; this file is the index to plan and track
> them. The *Status* column says where each row stands: `open`; `resolved (<date>)`, when a recorded result settles it
> (the evidence is in the row, the document's section and the streams' results files); or `dropped: <why>`, when it no
> longer applies. When an item is resolved or dropped, record the result in the document's section and set the row's
> status here; rows keep their numbers and order. Rows struck through before the status column existed stay struck.
